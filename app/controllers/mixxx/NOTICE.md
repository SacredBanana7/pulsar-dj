# Mixxx controller mappings

The `.midi.xml` and `.js` files in this folder are taken unchanged from the
[Mixxx](https://mixxx.org) project (144 mappings, commit `bc49961fd3e5a46cd46588090c652a0d7ec44e18`).

Copyright (C) 2001-2026 Mixxx Development Team and the individual mapping authors.
Licensed under the GNU General Public License, version 2 or (at your option) any later version.
Pulsar DJ runs them through its Mixxx compatibility layer (`app/js/mixxx/`).

Update with `node tools/sync-mixxx-mappings.mjs <mixxx checkout>`.
